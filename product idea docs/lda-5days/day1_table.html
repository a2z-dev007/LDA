# Activity Activity Type Linked to Day 5 Day 5 Section Name Score / Points How the Score Works What This Means (Plain English)
1 Connection Slider  —  User drags a number from 1 to 10 to show how connected they feel to their partner right now
Connection Slider Scoring Connection Score Section 1 — Mood Journey Chart Max 20 pts Higher the number dragged → more points. Score of 10 = full 20 pts. Score of 1 = just 2 pts. The number the user picks is the biggest single contributor to their Day 5 score. A person who feels more connected starts with more points.
Connection Slider Scoring Badge — Axis C Section 7 — Your Badge +2 pts to one side Score 6 or above = Protecting gets +2. Score 5 or below = Building gets +2. A higher score means the user feels their relationship is in a good place and wants to protect it. A lower score means they want to grow and build something better. This is the strongest single signal deciding whether the badge leans toward Protecting or Building.
Connection Slider Display Week Timeline + Letter Section 5 — Week in Moments + The Letter No score Number is shown as text. No calculation. "You came in at 6/10" appears on the Day 1 card in the timeline. The same number personalises the closing Letter on Day 5.
Connection Slider Scoring Trend Bonus Section 1 — Mood Journey (Bonus) Up to +10 bonus pts Day 1 score compared to Day 4 journal tone. Ended warmer = +10. About the same = +5. Ended lower = 0. Rewards a user who grew emotionally across the week. Day 1 score acts as the baseline that Day 4 is measured against.
2 The Honest Moment  —  A personalised, pre-written response shown based on the score. 4–6 second pause. No buttons. Nothing to tap.
The Honest Moment Experience Only No Day 5 link Does not appear in report No score No calculation. Nothing stored. This screen only exists to make the user feel understood before the quiz. A user who feels seen tends to answer the quiz more honestly — and those quiz answers DO feed the badge. The moment itself does nothing; what it enables matters.
3 Vibe Check — Game 05 (NEW)  —  8 emoji tiles showing relationship moods. User taps one. Options: Growing · Drifting · Passionate · Quiet · Tired · Hopeful · Tender · Energised
Vibe Check Scoring Connection Score Section 1 — Mood Journey Max 10 pts Positive tiles (Growing, Hopeful, Energised, Passionate) = 10 pts. Tender or Quiet = 7 pts. Heavy tiles (Tired, Drifting) = 3 pts. A positive relationship feeling contributes the most. A heavy feeling contributes less — but the user is still rewarded for showing up and being honest.
Vibe Check Scoring Badge — Axis A Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Tender or Quiet = Expressive +1. Energised, Passionate, or Growing = Active +1. The tile tapped hints at how the user expresses love. Quiet and tender vibes suggest someone who loves deeply and privately. Energised and passionate vibes suggest someone who expresses love through action.
Vibe Check Scoring Badge — Axis C Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Growing, Hopeful, Energised = Building +1. Tender or Quiet = Protecting +1. The vibe also hints at whether the user wants to grow the relationship further or protect what they already have.
Vibe Check Display Emotional Weather Section 2 — Emotional Weather No score Tile shown as a visual icon. No calculation. The Day 1 vibe tile is the first of three emotional snapshots shown in the Day 5 Emotional Weather section — sitting alongside the Day 2 mood and the Day 3 Mood Board theme.
4 Spark Quiz — 7 Questions  —  5 different question sets based on the Day 1 slider score. Each question shows two illustrated card options. Tap one, it auto-advances. No confirm button.
Spark Quiz (Q1 + Q6) Scoring Badge — Axis A Section 7 — Your Badge +2 pts per question Q1 Answer A = Expressive +2. Q1 Answer B = Active +2. Same logic applies to Q6. Q1 also breaks any tie on Axis A. Questions 1 and 6 are the two biggest inputs for deciding whether the badge leans Expressive or Active. Each one is worth double the smaller signals from other activities.
Spark Quiz (Q4 + Q7) Scoring Badge — Axis B Section 7 — Your Badge +2 pts per question Q4 Answer B = Deep +2. Q4 Answer A = Present +2. Same logic applies to Q7. Q7 also breaks any tie on Axis B. Questions 4 and 7 measure whether the user values depth of connection or living in the present moment. These are the two biggest Axis B inputs from Day 1.
Spark Quiz (Q5, Q6, Q7) Scoring Badge — Axis C Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt per question Q5-B = Protecting +1. Q5-A = Building +1. Q6-A = Protecting +1. Q7-A = Building +1. Three separate quiz answers each add a smaller signal to Axis C. Combined with the slider result (+2), these questions complete the full picture of whether the badge leans toward Protecting or Building.
Spark Quiz (Q2) Routes Day 2 Content Day 2 Follow-Up Question Day 2 — If mood = Frustrated No score Answer A on Q2 routes one follow-up question. Answer B routes a different one. Only activates if Day 2 mood is Frustrated. This is the only quiz answer carried forward to change what the user sees on Day 2. If they pick Frustrated on the Day 2 candle, their Q2 answer here decides exactly which follow-up question is shown to them.
5 Personality Type Result  —  One of four types shown: Steady Flame · Electric Spark · Deep Current · Shifting Tide. Comes with description, traits, and a growth area. Shareable card.
Personality Type Result Scoring Dedication Score Section 7 — Badge Tier +1 Dedication pt Completing all of Day 1 = 1 Dedication point. Dedication (max 7 pts total) feeds up to 30 of the 100 Connection Score points. Also sets badge tier: Gold (6–7 pts), Standard (4–5 pts), Emerging (2–3 pts). Finishing Day 1 earns the first Dedication point. Dedication is the highest-value component of the Connection Score — worth up to 30 points. It also decides whether the badge comes with a gold ring, a standard ring, or an emerging treatment.
Personality Type Result Routes Day 3 Content Day 3 Mirror Game Day 3 — 10 True/False Statements No score Type determines which set of 10 T/F statements is served. Steady Flame = small daily noticing. Electric Spark = dreams and ambition. Deep Current = inner life. Shifting Tide = relationship-specific. The personality type from Day 1 directly decides which 10 Mirror Game questions the user gets on Day 3. Two users with different types get a completely different set of questions. This is the longest-range direct connection in the entire 5-day flow.
Personality Type Result Routes Day 4 Content Day 4 Trivia Fact Day 4 — Closing Screen No score Type determines which psychology fact is shown at the end of Day 4. Different fact for each of the 4 types. The closing moment of Day 4 — a personalised psychology fact — is served based on the type assigned here on Day 1. The user never consciously connects these two moments. They just feel known.
Personality Type Result Display Type Deep Dive + Timeline + Letter Section 3 · Section 5 · The Letter No score Type name and pre-written content pulled and shown. No calculation. The type appears in three Day 5 places: Section 3 gives a full deep-dive with 3 strengths and 1 growth area. Section 5 Week in Moments shows the type on the Day 1 card. The personalised Letter references the type by name in the closing paragraph.
