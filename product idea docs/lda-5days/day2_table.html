BRIDGE 1→2  —  Activities the user completes before Day 2 begins. These happen on re-entry after Day 1 is done.
B1 Return Greeting + Streak Ring  —  "Welcome back." Streak ring shows Day 2. Day 1 recap card with personality type shown.
Return Greeting + Streak Ring Experience Only No Day 5 link Does not appear in report No score Nothing stored. No calculation. A purely motivational screen. Displays the Day 1 personality type as a recap. Its job is to remind the user what they built yesterday and bring them back into the experience with warmth.
B2 Intention Word Selector  —  "One word you want to bring into your relationship today." 8 options: Patient · Present · Honest · Warm · Playful · Open · Gentle · Brave. Tap = instant select.
Intention Word Selector Display Week Timeline Section 5 — Week in Moments (Day 2 card) No score Word is stored and pulled into display. No calculation. The selected word appears as a pill on the Day 2 card in the Week in Moments timeline on Day 5. It also appears in the Bridge 2→3 recap the following day. No score contribution — its purpose is emotional intention-setting before the Day 2 mood check-in.
B3 This or That: Us Edition — Game 06 (NEW)  —  3 rounds. Each round: two illustrated scenario cards (e.g. Beach vs Trek). User picks theirs, then predicts their partner's. Partner side sealed until they join.
This or That (G06) Scoring Badge — Axis A Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side 2 or more adventure/active picks across 3 rounds = Active +1. 2 or more comfort/home picks = Expressive +1. The type of scenario the user picks across the 3 rounds hints at how they show love — through action and energy (Active) or through comfort and closeness (Expressive).
This or That (G06) Scoring Badge — Axis C Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Predictions match own picks 2 or more times = Protecting +1. All 3 predictions are different from own picks = Building +1. If the user expects their partner to pick the same things as themselves, it signals they see the relationship as a shared identity (Protecting). If they expect differences, it signals they see their partner as distinct and are comfortable with that (Building).
This or That (G06) Display Couple Mode Unlock Section 8 — Couple Mode No score User's picks shown. Partner's side shown as sealed/locked. No calculation. On Day 5, the user sees their 3 picks with the partner's side still locked — "3 rounds waiting." When the partner joins and completes this activity, both sides merge and a Couple Card is generated showing what each person picked and where they matched.
DAY 2 MAIN ACTIVITIES  —  The Mood Room. Theme: Calm & Warm. Purpose: Emotional check-in · Deepen Day 1 reflection · Build private journal.
1 The Candle Mood Screen  —  A candle animates for 3 seconds, then 8 mood options appear: Distant · Connected · Grateful · Frustrated · Playful · Missed · Overwhelmed · Loved. Tap one. Candle flame changes. Auto-advances.
Candle Mood Scoring Connection Score Section 1 — Mood Journey Chart (D2 bar) Max 10 pts Connected / Loved / Playful = 9 (full score). Grateful / Missed = 6. Overwhelmed / Frustrated / Distant = 3. Normalised: 9→10 pts · 6→6 pts · 3→3 pts. The mood selected becomes the Day 2 bar on the Mood Journey chart. Warmer moods contribute more to the Connection Score. Even heavy moods contribute something — the user is rewarded for being honest.
Candle Mood Scoring Badge — Axis A Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Playful or Connected = Active +1. A playful or connected mood suggests someone who engages with their relationship energetically and outwardly — a signal that they lean Active in how they express love.
Candle Mood Scoring Badge — Axis B Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Loved / Grateful / Missed = Deep +1. Connected / Playful = Present +1. Moods like Loved, Grateful, or Missed suggest someone focused on deep, accumulated feeling. Moods like Connected or Playful suggest someone more focused on what is happening right now.
Candle Mood Scoring Badge — Axis C Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Grateful / Loved = Protecting +1. Frustrated / Distant / Overwhelmed = Building +1. Feeling grateful or loved suggests the user is in a good emotional place and wants to protect that. Feeling frustrated or distant suggests the relationship needs work — a Building signal.
Candle Mood Routes Day 2 Content Follow-Up Question Track Day 2 — Follow-Up Question (same day) No score Mood = Happiness track → one set of questions. Mood = Sadness track → another set. Mood = Frustrated → specific question based on Day 1 Quiz Q2 answer. The mood selected here immediately determines which follow-up question the user sees on the very next screen. Frustrated users get a question personalised further by their Day 1 quiz answer — the Day 1 → Day 2 connection in action.
Candle Mood Display Emotional Weather + Week Timeline Section 2 — Emotional Weather · Section 5 — Week in Moments No score Mood icon shown in report. No calculation. The Day 2 mood icon appears as the second of three emotional snapshots in the Emotional Weather section. It also shows on the Day 2 card in the Week in Moments timeline.
2 One Good Thing  —  "Name one thing about your partner that made you feel something good recently. It can be tiny." Short open text (10–80 characters). Card flies into the journal on save. Optional.
One Good Thing Scoring Badge — Axis A Section 7 — Your Badge Partial signal Response is 40 or more characters = Expressive signal (partial contribution toward Axis A). A generous, considered response (40+ characters) suggests the user is emotionally expressive and willing to put feeling into words — a signal toward the Expressive side of Axis A.
One Good Thing Display Week Timeline + Bridge 2→3 recap Section 5 — Week in Moments (Day 2 card) No score Exact words shown. No calculation. The user's exact words appear on the Day 2 card in the Week in Moments timeline on Day 5. The same words also appear the very next day in the Bridge 2→3 recap — "He made chai without asking. That's the whole thing really." — creating a feeling that the app is listening.
3 The Follow-Up Question  —  One open-text question. Adapts based on mood selected + Day 1 Quiz Q2 answer (for Frustrated track). Privacy note visible. Optional.
Follow-Up Question Scoring Dedication Score Section 7 — Badge Tier +1 Dedication pt Day 2 complete (mood answered + follow-up answered, not skipped) = +1 Dedication point. Finishing Day 2 properly earns 1 Dedication point. Dedication drives up to 30 of 100 Connection Score points and determines badge tier (gold, standard, emerging).
Follow-Up Question Scoring Badge — Axis A Section 7 — Your Badge +2 pts Response is 60 or more characters = Expressive +2. This is the highest character-count signal in the entire 5-day app. A long, considered response to this question is the single strongest Expressive signal in the whole flow. Someone who writes 60+ characters here is clearly comfortable expressing themselves in words — a defining Expressive trait.
Follow-Up Question Display Week Timeline Section 5 — Week in Moments (Day 2 card) No score Exact words shown. No calculation. If skipped, only the mood name shows — no personal quote. The user's answer appears on the Day 2 card in the Week in Moments timeline. If they skipped this question, the card shows only the mood name with no personal quote — a gentle incentive to answer.
