How to Read This Document

🟢 Scoring Activity

Directly contributes a number to the Day 5 scorecard — either to the Connection Score, the Dedication Score, or to determining the user's Badge type.

🔵 Display-Feed Activity

Doesn't change any score number, but the user's response populates visible sections of the Day 5 report — their exact words, choices, or tiles shown back to them.

🟣 Experience-Only

Pure emotional moment. Nothing is stored, nothing feeds Day 5. These screens exist only to create a feeling — priming, warmth, or reflection — before the next activity.

The core principle: Every scoring activity across Days 1–4 quietly builds the Day 5 report in the background. By Day 5, the app already knows the user's badge type, connection score, emotional arc, relationship needs, and personality profile — all from what they did, not what they wrote.

DAY 1 — The Spark Check

Theme: Bold & Fun Purpose: Set baseline · Generate personality type · Earn partner invite right
Connection Slider
User drags 1–10 to express how connected they feel right now
🟢 Scoring
Immediately: The score splits users into 1 of 5 emotional segments. Each segment gets a completely different set of 7 quiz questions — written specifically for where they are emotionally.
Day 5 Connection Score: The starting score contributes the largest single chunk (up to 20 of 100 points). A score of 10 contributes max; a score of 1 contributes the least.
Day 5 Badge Axis C (Protecting vs Building): A score of 6 or above pushes the badge toward "Protecting what you have." A score of 5 or below pushes it toward "Building something new." This is the single strongest signal for Axis C.
Day 5 Report: The number appears in the Mood Journey chart as the Day 1 bar, in the Week in Moments card ("You came in at 6/10"), and in the personalised Letter.
Day 5 Trend Bonus: The Day 1 score is compared against the emotional tone of Day 4's journal entry. If the user ended warmer than they started, they earn bonus points on the Connection Score.
The Honest Moment
A personalised response to their slider score — pre-written, segment-adaptive. 4–6 second pause.
🟣 Experience
No data stored. No Day 5 contribution. This screen exists purely to make the user feel seen before the quiz begins — a low scorer hears "Most people wouldn't admit this. You just did. That takes courage." A high scorer hears something that gently challenges their perfect 10. The emotional tone this sets directly influences how honestly users answer the quiz, which DOES feed the badge.
Vibe Check NEW · Game 05
8 emoji tiles showing different relationship moods (Growing, Drifting, Passionate, Quiet, Tired, Hopeful, Tender, Energised). User taps one.
🟢 Scoring
Day 5 Connection Score: Positive vibes (Growing, Hopeful, Energised, Passionate) contribute the most — up to 10 points. Tender or Quiet vibes contribute 7. Heavy vibes (Tired, Drifting) contribute 3.
Day 5 Badge Axis A (Expressive vs Active): Tender/Quiet vibes suggest someone who loves quietly → Expressive. Energised/Passionate/Growing suggest someone who shows love through doing → Active.
Day 5 Badge Axis C: Growing/Hopeful/Energised → Building (wants more). Tender/Quiet → Protecting (wants to preserve what's there).
Day 5 Report Section 2 (Emotional Weather): The Day 1 vibe tile appears as the first of three emotional snapshots of the week — shown visually alongside Day 2 mood and Day 3 Mood Board theme.
Spark Quiz — 7 Questions
Segment-adaptive — 5 different question sets. Each question is two illustrated card options. No confirm button, auto-advances.
🟢 Scoring
Immediately: All 7 answers are combined to calculate the user's personality type (Steady Flame, Electric Spark, Deep Current, or Shifting Tide). This happens silently in the background.
Day 5 Badge — All 3 Axes: Specific quiz answers directly feed the Expressive/Active, Deep/Present, and Protecting/Building axes. Questions 1, 4, 5, 6, and 7 each carry weight toward specific badge outcomes. Question 1 also acts as a tie-breaker for Axis A. Question 7 breaks Axis B ties.
Day 2 Follow-up Question: One specific quiz answer (Question 2) is held forward to Day 2 — if the user selects "Frustrated" as their Day 2 mood, the follow-up question they see is customised based on whether they said "small things pile up" or "one big thing" in that answer.
Personality Type Result
One of four types displayed with description, traits, and a growth area. Shareable card. CTAs: Save / Come back tomorrow.
🟢 Scoring 🔵 Display-Feed
Dedication Score: Completing Day 1 fully earns +1 point toward the Dedication Score (which directly drives 30 points of the Connection Score and determines badge tier).
Day 3 Mirror Game — Direct Route: The personality type determines which of 5 sets of 10 True/False statements is served on Day 3. Steady Flame gets statements about small daily noticing. Electric Spark gets statements about dreams and ambition. Deep Current gets statements about inner life. Shifting Tide gets relationship-specific statements. This is the most direct Day 1 → Day 3 connection in the entire app.
Day 4 Trivia Fact: A personalised psychology fact, specific to the personality type, is served as the closing moment of Day 4.
Day 5 Report Section 3: The Personality Type Deep Dive section pulls this type and shows 3 pre-written strengths + 1 growth area. It also appears in the Week in Moments D1 card and in the personalised Letter.

BRIDGE 1→2 — Entering Day 2

3 Activities
Return Greeting + Streak Ring
"Welcome back." Streak ring shows Day 2. Day 1 recap card with personality type.
🟣 Experience
Purely motivational. Displays the personality type from Day 1 as a recap. No new data stored, no score contribution. The streak ring begins building the identity of "someone who comes back."
Intention Word Selector
"One word you want to bring into your relationship today." 8 options: Patient / Present / Honest / Warm / Playful / Open / Gentle / Brave.
🔵 Display-Feed
No score contribution. The chosen word travels forward: shown as a pill in the Bridge 2→3 recap, and appears on the Day 2 card in the Day 5 Week in Moments timeline. Its purpose is emotional intention-setting — priming the user for the Day 2 mood check-in.
This or That: Us Edition NEW · Game 06
3 rounds. Each round: two illustrated scenario cards (e.g. Beach vs Trek). User picks theirs, then predicts their partner's. Partner side sealed.
🟢 Scoring
Day 5 Badge Axis A: If the user consistently picks adventure/active options across 2 or more rounds → Active signal. Consistently picking comfort/home options → Expressive signal.
Day 5 Badge Axis C: If their predictions match their own picks 2+ times (they expect their partner to be like them) → Protecting. If all 3 predictions differ from their own picks (they know their partner is different) → Building.
Day 5 Couple Mode Section: The user's picks are shown — partner's side remains sealed with a lock icon. "3 rounds waiting" status displayed as an open loop. When partner joins: both sides merge, prediction accuracy is calculated, and a Couple Card is generated ("You picked Beach. They picked Trek.").
🔒 The partner's sealed side is never shown back to the solo user again until the partner completes this same activity on their own device.

DAY 2 — The Mood Room

Theme: Calm & Warm Purpose: Emotional check-in · Deepen Day 1 reflection · Build private journal
The Candle Mood Screen
A candle animates for 3 seconds, then an 8-mood grid appears: Distant · Connected · Grateful · Frustrated · Playful · Missed · Overwhelmed · Loved. Tap selects; candle flame changes appearance.
🟢 Scoring
Immediately routes to the right follow-up question: The mood selected places the user on a Happiness track (Connected/Grateful/Playful/Loved), a Sadness track (Distant/Missed/Overwhelmed), or a Frustrated track. Each track has 3 different rotating follow-up questions.
Day 5 Connection Score: Mood score contributes up to 10 points. Connected/Loved/Playful = highest; Grateful/Missed = mid; Overwhelmed/Frustrated/Distant = lowest.
Day 5 Badge — All 3 Axes: Playful or Connected → Active signal. Loved/Grateful/Missed → Deep signal. Connected/Playful → Present signal. Grateful/Loved → Protecting. Frustrated/Distant/Overwhelmed → Building.
Day 5 Report Section 2 (Emotional Weather): The Day 2 mood icon appears as the second tile in the 3-tile Emotional Weather display. Also feeds the Mood Journey chart as the Day 2 bar.
Bridge 3→4 Conditional: If the same mood is selected for 3 consecutive days, a gentle acknowledgement line is added: "You've been feeling [mood] for a few days. That's worth noticing. The jar is a good place for that."
One Good Thing
"Name one thing about your partner that made you feel something good recently. It can be tiny." Short open text (10–80 characters). Card animates into the journal icon on save.
🟢 Scoring 🔵 Display-Feed
Day 5 Badge Axis A (partial signal): If the user writes 40 or more characters — meaning they gave a considered, generous response — it registers as an Expressive signal.
Bridge 2→3 Recap: Their exact words are shown back on the bridge card — "He made chai without asking. That's the whole thing really." — creating a sense of continuity and that the app is listening.
Day 5 Week in Moments — Day 2 Card: Exact words shown in the timeline.
The Follow-Up Question
One open-text question, adaptive to mood track + Day 1 quiz answer (for Frustrated track). Privacy note visible. Optional.
🟢 Scoring
Dedication Score: Completing Day 2 fully (mood + this follow-up, not skipped) earns +1 point to the Dedication Score.
Day 5 Badge Axis A: If the user writes 60 or more characters in their response, it signals they are an Expressive communicator — contributing +2 to the Expressive side of Axis A. This is the highest character-count signal in the entire app.
Day 5 Week in Moments — Day 2 Card: Exact words shown. If skipped, the card only shows the mood name — no personal quote.
The D1 → D2 connection in action: If Day 1 quiz question 2 was answered as "small things pile up" AND the user selects Frustrated on Day 2, they see: "What's the specific small thing that tipped it today? There always is one." If they answered "one big thing," they see: "Is this the same thing again — or something new? Knowing which one changes everything."

DAY 3 — The Mirror Game

Theme: Playful + Provocative Strongest conversion day · Most partner invite hooks · Highest retention
The Appreciation Snap
"Before we test what you know about them — what do you want them to know about you right now?" One sentence, open text.
🟢 Scoring 🔵 Display-Feed
Dedication Score: +0.5 points toward the Dedication Score.
Day 5 Week in Moments — Day 3 Card: Exact words shown. If skipped, the slot reads "Day 3 — you noticed them" with no quote. Purpose: self-expressive moment before the partner-focused T/F game begins. The arc goes: "I want them to know this" → "How well do I actually know them?"
Finish My Sentence NEW · Game 02
A sentence stem appears in large italic. User picks one of 4 illustrated tap cards to complete it. No writing. Partner's version of the same stem is shown as a sealed/locked card beside theirs.
🟢 Scoring
Day 5 Badge — All 3 Axes: Each of the 4 completion cards carries a hidden emotional tag (e.g. "resilience," "playful," "deep," "protective"). The tag of the selected card feeds all three badge axes simultaneously — it is one of the richest single-tap signals in the app for badge determination.
Dedication Score: +0.5 points.
Day 5 Week in Moments — Day 3 Card: The selected sentence completion is shown in Playfair italic.
Day 5 Couple Mode Section: The stem and the user's answer are shown — partner's sealed card beside it. "1 of 2 sides complete" status. When partner joins and completes Day 3: both sides reveal simultaneously — described as the "Sentence Reveal" moment in the couple dashboard.
Mirror Game — 10 True/False Statements
"You think you know your partner. Let's test that." One statement per full screen. Tap TRUE or FALSE. Auto-advances. 10-pip progress bar.
🟢 Scoring
⚡ Key Day 1 → Day 3 connection: The specific 10 statements shown here are 100% determined by the personality type calculated on Day 1. A Steady Flame gets statements about small daily noticing habits. An Electric Spark gets statements about their partner's dreams and ambitions. A Deep Current gets questions about their partner's private interior life. A Shifting Tide gets relationship-specific statements about honesty, choice, and effort.
Day 5 Connection Score — Partner Knowledge component: The count of TRUE answers (out of 10) contributes directly. Up to 10 points.
Day 5 Badge Axis B (Deep vs Present): Answering TRUE to 7 or more statements suggests someone deeply invested in knowing their partner → Deep +2. Answering TRUE to 4 or fewer suggests someone more focused on the present moment than accumulated knowledge → Present +2.
Day 5 Badge Axis C: 7 or more TRUE answers → Protecting signal (you know what you have and want to hold it).
Day 5 Partner Knowledge Score: Shown directly as a /10 score. When partner joins, the score is recalibrated — showing what % of the user's solo predictions actually matched their partner's own self-reported answers.
Bridge 3→4: "Yesterday you were 7 for 10" shown in the recap card.
Mirror Results — Open Loop
Split screen: user's 10 answers fully visible on left. Partner's side completely frosted + locked on right. Invite CTA. Pre-written WhatsApp message. Screenshot icon.
🟣 Experience
The most important conversion screen in the entire app. No new scoring happens here. The user has invested 3 minutes answering questions about their partner and cannot see whether they were right. The only resolution is inviting the partner. If user taps Invite: this is recorded and the Bridge 3→4 adapts accordingly.
Mood Board Match NEW · Game 07
"Pick 3 tiles that describe your relationship this week." 10 illustrated icon tiles (Comfort, Fresh start, Our world, Under cloud, Intimate, Peaceful, Up & down, Figuring out, Blossoming, Safe home). Save unlocks after exactly 3 selected.
🟢 Scoring
Day 5 Connection Score — Mood Board Positivity: Each tile carries a hidden emotional tag (Present, Protecting, Deep, or Building). The proportion of "positive" tiles (Present or Protecting) among the 3 chosen contributes up to 10 points to the Connection Score.
Day 5 Badge — All 3 Axes simultaneously: The majority tag across the 3 tiles chosen determines the axis contribution for all three axes. This is the only activity in the app that influences all 3 badge axes from a single gesture.
Dedication Score: +0.5 points.
Day 5 Report Section 2 (Emotional Weather): The 3 selected tiles are grouped into one of 5 named themes (Safe & Steady, Growing & Open, Navigating Together, Deeply Connected, Mixed & Moving). This theme name appears as the Day 3 panel in the Emotional Weather section.
Day 5 Week in Moments — Day 3 Card: The 3 tile icons shown.
Placement rationale: The Mirror Game surfaces uncertainty ("I wasn't sure about some of those"). The Mood Board immediately converts that uncertainty into a visual emotional reframe. The arc: analytical (T/F) → visual (tiles) → anchoring (One Certainty). This is why Day 3 has the highest retention rate.
The One Certainty
"Despite everything you're not sure about — what's the one thing you know for certain?" Open text. ~15 seconds.
🟢 Scoring 🔵 Display-Feed
Dedication Score: +0.5 points (contributing toward the D3 complete bonus).
Bridge 3→4: Shown as Card 2 in the recap — exact words in Playfair italic. This is the emotional anchor the user carries into Day 4.
Day 5 Week in Moments — Day 3 Card: Exact words shown.
The Letter (Day 5 fallback): If the user skipped the Day 4 Memory Jar, the Letter template uses this certainty text instead as the personal moment referenced.

DAY 4 — The Memory Jar

Theme: Warm & Emotional Purpose: Preservation · Surface unspoken needs · Build journaling habit
The Memory Jar
Jar shown at 60% fill with 3 notes visible. "Drop one memory here." User chooses input type: Text, Photo, or Emoji + date. Drop arc animation on save.
🟢 Scoring 🔵 Display-Feed
Dedication Score: Dropping a memory earns +1 point — the single largest Dedication Score contribution from any Day 4 activity.
Day 5 Badge Axis A: The input type signals love expression style. Writing a text memory → Expressive +2 (the highest single signal for Expressive in the whole app). Dropping a photo → Active +2. Using emoji + date → Active +1.
Day 5 Section 6 (Jar Full Reveal): The memory content appears as a note inside the jar at 100% fill.
The Letter (Day 5): The first 8 words of the memory are pulled into the personalised letter template: "[Name]. You started this week at a 6. The [Steady Flame] in you knew something worth protecting was here. '[The day we got caught in the rain]…' That's the memory you chose to keep."
Bridge 4→5: First 60 characters of the memory shown in the ceremonial recap card.
The Tiny Compliment
"One word that describes how your partner makes you feel." 8 word pills: Seen / Safe / Lighter / Lucky / Proud / Loved / Calm / Home. Or free-type up to 10 characters.
🟢 Scoring 🔵 Display-Feed
Dedication Score: +0.5 points.
Day 5 Jar: The word glows inside the jar as a floating label on the full reveal. Week in Moments D4 card: word shown. Shareable card: word shown as a glowing pill. No badge axis contribution — this is purely emotional and display-driven.
Priority Shuffle NEW · Game 08
"5 cards. Tap your top 3 — in order of what you need most right now." Cards: More warmth & affection · Dedicated time together · More calm, less stress · Laughter & lightness · Deeper conversations. No writing at all.
🟢 Scoring
Day 5 Badge Axis B (Deep vs Present): Picking "Deeper conversations" anywhere in the top 3 → Deep +2. "Laughter & lightness" as the top pick → Present +2. "More calm, less stress" in the picks → Deep +1.
Day 5 Badge Axis C: Top pick of "Warmth" or "Calm" → Protecting +2 (wants to restore safety). Top pick of "Laughter," "Time," or "Conversations" → Building +2 (wants to create something new).
Dedication Score: +0.5 points.
Day 5 Section 4 (Relationship Needs): This is the only activity that gets its own dedicated report section. The top pick is displayed prominently with a pre-written one-line description. All 3 picks shown in ranked order. Partner's picks shown as sealed until they join.
Bridge 4→5 — New Card 3: "Your top need this week: [Warmth & affection]" shown with the line "Day 5 shows you if they felt the same." — creates a specific open loop anticipation going into the final day.
The Daily 2
Two journal cards. Card 1: "What emotion are you carrying into today?" Card 2: "What's something your relationship taught you this week?" Both open text, both optional.
🟢 Scoring 🔵 Display-Feed
Dedication Score: Both answered = +0.5. Only one answered = +0.25.
Day 5 Badge Axis A: If both answers are substantial (40+ characters each) → Expressive +1. Signals a reflective, verbally expressive person.
Day 5 Connection Score — Mood Chart D4 Bar: Card 1's text is scanned for emotional tone keywords. Warmth words (grateful, happy, calm, loved, good) score 7. Heavy words (tired, frustrated, anxious, distant) score 4. Neutral = 5. This becomes the Day 4 bar on the Mood Journey chart.
Day 5 Connection Score — Trend Bonus: The Day 4 tone is compared to the Day 1 slider score. If the user ended emotionally warmer than they started → +10 bonus points. Equal → +5. Declining → +0. This is the arc reward.
Day 5 Week in Moments — Day 4 Card: Card 2's answer shown in exact words.
Love Drop + Drop Box UPDATED · Game 04
Two tabs. Love Drop (new): Leave a sealed message for your partner — choose from 4 types (compliment, memory, challenge, something I've been meaning to say) with zero-typing preset options. Drop Box (existing): Write something hard — AI reframes it. Original deleted.
🟢 Scoring
Day 5 Badge — Love Drop: Leaving a compliment → Expressive signal (Axis A). Using the Love Drop at all → Deep signal (Axis B). Leaving a challenge → Building (Axis C). Leaving a compliment or memory → Protecting (Axis C).
Day 5 Badge — Drop Box: Using the Drop Box → Deep +2 (highest single Deep signal in the app). Also → Building +1 (Axis C). Suggests someone willing to do the hard emotional work.
Dedication Score: Love Drop used = +0.5. Drop Box used = +0.5. Maximum +1.0 from this screen.
Day 5 Jar Reveal: If Love Drop was used → a gold sealed note appears in the jar: "Something waiting for them." If Drop Box was used → a rose note: "Something you found the words for." Both are visible and tappable inside the jar.
When Partner Joins: Love Drop content unseals on the partner's device — they find the message. This is the most intimate Couple Mode moment in the app.

Full screen for 5 seconds. A relationship psychology fact tailored to the user's personality type. Swipe up to dismiss. No CTA.
🟣 Experience

WHAT DAY 5 SCORES — 4 Numbers, What Builds Them

Connection Score · /100

A composite number capped at 100. Built from 7 components. Displayed prominently on the report card.

Day 1 slider → up to 20 pts (the single biggest component)
Day 1 Vibe Check → up to 10 pts
Day 2 mood → up to 10 pts
Day 3 Mood Board → up to 10 pts
Day 3 Mirror Game → up to 10 pts
Dedication Score → up to 30 pts
Day 4 Trend Bonus → up to 10 pts

Dedication Score · /7

Measures commitment across all 5 days. Drives 30 of the 100 Connection Score points AND determines badge tier (gold / standard / emerging).

D1 fully complete → +1
D2 complete, not skipped → +1
D3 complete (T/F + Mood Board + FMS) → +1 (+0.5 each for MB & FMS)
D4 memory dropped → +1
D4 Priority Shuffle, Love Drop, Daily 2, Drop Box → +0.5 each
D5 Promise completed → +1
Skip never used across all days → +1
Max possible: 9 → capped at 7.0
6–7 pts = Gold tier · 4–5 = Standard · 2–3 = Emerging

Partner Knowledge Score · /10

Direct output of the Day 3 Mirror Game. The count of TRUE answers out of 10. Displayed on the report as-is.

When partner joins: the score is recalibrated — the number shown updates to reflect what % of the solo predictions actually matched what the partner said about themselves.

The only score that changes after Day 5 — everything else locks when the report generates.

THE SEGMENT JOURNEY — How Day 1's Slider Score Shapes All 5 Days

The Day 1 slider score is the single most impactful input in the entire 5-day experience. Here is how it cascades forward:

D1 · Instant
Slider score → 1 of 5 segments
Segment determines which 7 quiz questions are shown. A score of 1–2 gets questions about loneliness and courage. A score of 10 gets questions that gently examine whether "perfect" is really perfect.
D1 · 30 sec later

Personality type → trivia fact
The closing gift of Day 4 is a psychology fact specific to how the user's type loves. Steady Flames get one fact. Electric Sparks get another. The user never knows — they just feel known.

GAPS & CONNECTIONS TO ADD

Founder action items

The following connections currently exist as logic or data but are not yet surfaced visibly in the experience. These are opportunities to make the user feel more known:

Gap 1 — Mood continuity not yet visible to user

The 5-day mood chart is calculated and shown on Day 5 — but the user has no visual sense of their emotional arc while they're in it (Days 2–4). Consider: a subtle, always-visible "emotional pulse" that grows across the days, so users feel the arc building before Day 5.

Gap 2 — Segment identity never reflected back before Day 5

The user is placed in a segment ("The Slow Drift," "The Strong Foundation") on Day 1 but never told what segment they're in. The Personality Type is revealed, but the emotional starting point is not explicitly named. Consider: a soft, non-clinical acknowledgement on Day 2 or 3 that references where they started — "You came in feeling like things needed work. Look what three days of showing up feels like."

Gap 3 — Personality type personalisation stops after Day 4 Trivia

After the Day 1 type determination, personalisation happens in Day 3 Mirror Game questions (strong connection) and Day 4 Trivia (good connection) — but the Bridge 3→4 quote, the Bridge 4→5 Significance Line, and several Day 5 insight lines are still generic. Consider: one type-adaptive phrase in at least Bridge 3→4 and one in the Day 5 report beyond just Section 3.

Gap 4 — Daily 2 keyword tone scoring is invisible

The Day 4 journal entry (Card 1: "What emotion are you carrying?") is scanned for warmth vs heavy keywords to set the D4 bar on the mood chart and determine the Trend Bonus. The user has no idea this is happening. While intentionally invisible, it could be worth surfacing on Day 5 as a gentle reflection: "The way you described Day 4 felt warmer than how you described Day 1."

Gap 5 — Bridge quotes are fully generic

All 4 bridge quotes ("Noticing what's good is not the same as ignoring what's hard", etc.) are the same for all users regardless of their segment, personality type, or preceding day's activity. These are good quotes — but they could be even more powerful with 2–3 variations per bridge that adapt to the user's emotional segment or recent mood pattern.

Opportunity — D5 report pattern reads need more combinations mapped

The Emotional Weather section in the Day 5 report currently has 4 pre-written pattern reads (all positive, mixed, heavy-then-positive, consistently heavy). The actual combinations possible across 3 inputs (Day 1 vibe, Day 2 mood, Day 3 Mood Board theme) run into dozens. Mapping at least 8–10 combinations with specific language would meaningfully increase the "they know me" feeling at the report's most personal section.
