DAY 3 MAIN ACTIVITIES  —  The Mirror Game. Theme: Playful + Provocative. Purpose: Strongest conversion day · Most partner invite hooks · Highest retention.
1 The Appreciation Snap  —  "Before we test what you know about them — what do you want them to know about you right now?" One open sentence. Card animates into the journal on save. Optional.
Appreciation Snap Scoring Dedication Score Section 7 — Badge Tier +0.5 Dedication pts Completing this screen (not skipping) = +0.5 Dedication points toward the Day 3 complete bonus. Counts toward the Day 3 Dedication point. Day 3 is fully complete when all three Day 3 sub-activities (Appreciation Snap + Mirror Game + Mood Board + FMS) are done — together earning 1 full Dedication point.
Appreciation Snap Display Week Timeline Section 5 — Week in Moments (Day 3 card) No score Exact words shown. No calculation. If skipped, slot reads "Day 3 — you noticed them" with no personal quote. The user's exact words are shown on the Day 3 card in the Week in Moments timeline. This is the only self-expressive moment before the Mirror Game — its arc: "What I want them to know" → "How well do I actually know them?"
2 Finish My Sentence — Game 02 (NEW)  —  A sentence stem appears ("The thing I love most about us is…"). User picks one of 4 illustrated tap cards to complete it. No typing. Partner's version is shown as a sealed/locked card beside theirs.
Finish My Sentence (G02) Scoring Badge — Axis A Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Card tag = Expressive or Deep → Expressive +1. Card tag = Active or Playful → Active +1. Each of the 4 completion cards carries a hidden emotional tag. Picking a card about quiet safety or deep knowing suggests Expressive. Picking a card about laughter or doing things together suggests Active.
Finish My Sentence (G02) Scoring Badge — Axis B Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Card tag = Deep or Resilience → Deep +1. Card tag = Present or Playful → Present +1. Choosing a card about getting through hard times together or knowing each other deeply suggests Deep. Choosing a card about laughter or ordinary present moments suggests Present.
Finish My Sentence (G02) Scoring Badge — Axis C Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side Card tag = Protective → Protecting +1. Card tag = Building or Active → Building +1. A card about safety and protection suggests the user wants to guard what they have. A card about growth, action, or something new suggests Building.
Finish My Sentence (G02) Scoring Dedication Score Section 7 — Badge Tier +0.5 Dedication pts Completing this screen = +0.5 Dedication points. Counts toward the Day 3 Dedication point alongside the Appreciation Snap and Mood Board.
Finish My Sentence (G02) Display Week Timeline + Couple Mode Unlock Section 5 — Week in Moments · Section 8 — Couple Mode No score Selected sentence shown in report. Partner's sealed card shown alongside in Section 8. No calculation. The selected sentence appears in Playfair italic on the Day 3 timeline card. In Section 8, the user's answer is visible with the partner's side sealed — "1 of 2 sides complete." When partner joins and completes Day 3, both sides reveal simultaneously.
3 Mirror Game — 10 True/False Statements  —  "You think you know your partner. Let's test that." One statement per full screen. Tap TRUE or FALSE. Auto-advances. Progress bar at bottom. Statement set is determined by Day 1 personality type.
Mirror Game T/F Scoring Connection Score Section 1 — Mood Journey (Partner Knowledge component) Max 10 pts Number of TRUE answers out of 10. Each TRUE answer = 1 point. Maximum 10 pts. The count of TRUE answers directly contributes to the Connection Score. It also becomes the Partner Knowledge Score shown on the Day 5 report. When the partner joins, this score is recalibrated — showing what percentage of the user's predictions actually matched what the partner said about themselves.
Mirror Game T/F Scoring Badge — Axis B Section 7 — Your Badge +2 pts to one side 7 to 10 TRUE answers = Deep +2. 0 to 4 TRUE answers = Present +2. Answering TRUE to most statements suggests someone deeply invested in knowing their partner's inner world — a Deep signal. Answering FALSE to most suggests someone more focused on the present and less certain about accumulated depth — a Present signal.
Mirror Game T/F Scoring Badge — Axis C Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt 7 or more TRUE answers = Protecting +1. A user who answers TRUE to 7 or more statements believes they know a lot about their partner — suggesting they feel the relationship is solid and worth protecting.
Mirror Game T/F Display Partner Knowledge Score Section 7 — Your Badge (sub-score display) No score (displayed as-is) TRUE count shown directly as X out of 10. The Partner Knowledge Score is shown on the Day 5 report as a standalone figure alongside the Connection Score. It is the only score that can change after Day 5 — when the partner joins, accuracy is recalibrated based on their actual answers.
4 Mirror Results — Open Loop  —  Split screen. User's 10 answers visible on the left. Partner's side completely frosted and locked on the right. Invite CTA. Pre-written WhatsApp message. Screenshot icon.
Mirror Results Screen Experience Only No Day 5 score link Does not score · Records partner invite if tapped No score No calculation. If user taps Invite Partner, that action is recorded and Bridge 3→4 adapts accordingly. This is the most important conversion screen in the entire app. No new scoring happens here. The user has invested 3 minutes answering questions and cannot see if they were right — the only resolution is inviting the partner. The open loop is intentional.
5 Mood Board Match — Game 07 (NEW)  —  "Pick 3 tiles that describe your relationship this week." 10 illustrated icon tiles to choose from: Comfort · Fresh start · Our world · Under cloud · Intimate · Peaceful · Up & down · Figuring out · Blossoming · Safe home. Save unlocks only when exactly 3 are selected.
Mood Board Match (G07) Scoring Connection Score Section 1 — Mood Journey (Mood Board component) Max 10 pts Each tile carries a hidden tag: Present, Protecting, Deep, or Building. Count of tiles tagged Present or Protecting ÷ 3 × 10 = points. E.g. 2 warm tiles = 6.7 pts. 3 warm tiles = 10 pts. The 3 tiles chosen contribute to the Connection Score based on their emotional warmth. Tiles like Comfort, Safe Home, and Peaceful are tagged Present or Protecting (warm). Tiles like Up & down and Figuring out are tagged Building (growth-oriented, less warm). More warm tiles = more points.
Mood Board Match (G07) Scoring Badge — Axis A Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side 2 or more of the 3 tiles carry an Expressive tag = Expressive +1. 2 or more carry an Active tag = Active +1. The majority tag across the 3 chosen tiles decides the Axis A signal. Tiles like Comfort and Intimate lean Expressive. Tiles like Fresh Start and Blossoming lean Active.
Mood Board Match (G07) Scoring Badge — Axis B Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side 2 or more tiles carry a Deep tag = Deep +1. 2 or more carry a Present tag = Present +1. Tiles like Our World and Intimate lean Deep. Tiles like Comfort and Peaceful lean Present. The majority across 3 chosen tiles decides.
Mood Board Match (G07) Scoring Badge — Axis C Section 7 — Your Badge +1 pt to one side 2 or more tiles carry a Protecting tag = Protecting +1. 2 or more carry a Building tag = Building +1. This is the only single activity in the entire app that simultaneously feeds all 3 badge axes from one gesture. The 3 tiles chosen in under 30 seconds shape three separate badge decisions at once.
Mood Board Match (G07) Scoring Dedication Score Section 7 — Badge Tier +0.5 Dedication pts Completing this screen = +0.5 Dedication points. Counts toward the Day 3 Dedication point. Together with Appreciation Snap (+0.5) and Finish My Sentence (+0.5), Day 3 is capable of contributing 1.5 Dedication points when all three are completed.
Mood Board Match (G07) Display Emotional Weather + Week Timeline Section 2 — Emotional Weather · Section 5 — Week in Moments No score 3 tile icons and theme name shown in report. No calculation. The 3 tiles are grouped into one of 5 named emotional themes (Safe & Steady, Growing & Open, Navigating Together, Deeply Connected, Mixed & Moving). This theme appears as the Day 3 panel in the Emotional Weather section of the report.
6 The One Certainty  —  "Despite everything you're not sure about — what's the one thing you know for certain?" Open text. Approximately 15 seconds. Positioned after the Mood Board — converting uncertainty back to warmth. Optional.
The One Certainty Scoring Dedication Score Section 7 — Badge Tier +0.5 Dedication pts Completing this screen = +0.5 Dedication points. Counts toward the Day 3 Dedication total. Also earns the Day 3 complete bonus when combined with the other Day 3 sub-activities.
The One Certainty Display Week Timeline + Bridge 3→4 + The Letter (fallback) Section 5 — Week in Moments (Day 3 card) No score Exact words shown in 3 places. No calculation. The user's exact words appear on: (1) the Bridge 3→4 recap the following day as an emotional anchor into Day 4, (2) the Day 3 card in the Week in Moments timeline, and (3) the personalised Letter on Day 5 — but only as a fallback if the Day 4 memory was skipped.
