This or That: Us Edition — Intention-Word Adaptive Questions

When a user selects their intention word in Bridge 1→2, the This or That game that immediately follows serves questions directly rooted in that word's emotional territory. The user chose "Patient" because something in their relationship needs patience right now — so every question they get lives in that space. The game feels like it's listening, not just playing.

🕰 Patient
Intention: Patient
They chose this word because something in the relationship needs more time, space, or tolerance today. Questions live in the territory of waiting, processing, and giving each other room.
"You chose patience today. These 3 rounds are about how you and your partner give each other space — and whether they'd say the same."
Round 1
"When I'm waiting for my partner to come around on something important to me…"
🌊
I give it time without saying more
If it matters, they'll get there
VS
🗣️
I say it one more time, clearly, then let it sit
I need to know they heard me before I let go
Axis A

🌊 Silent wait = Expressive (feels inward) · 🗣️ One more say = Active (needs to express)

Couple Card

"You wait in silence. They wait by speaking. Both of you are being patient — just differently."

Round 2
"When my partner is taking longer to process something emotionally…"
🤫
I give them space and wait for them to come to me
Hovering makes it worse
VS
🫂
I check in gently, even if they haven't asked
I'd rather interrupt than leave them alone in it
Axis A

🤫 Space = Expressive · 🫂 Check in = Active

Couple Card

"You give space. They come closer. Between you — no one is ever really alone."

Round 3
"When things between us are going through a slow, quiet patch…"
🌱
I trust it — every relationship has seasons
Quiet doesn't mean something is wrong
VS
💬
I name it — "are we okay?" even if it's awkward
Silence that isn't named starts to feel like distance
Axis A

🌱 Trust it = Expressive · 💬 Name it = Active

Couple Card

"You trust the quiet. They can't rest until it's named. You've been okay all along — you just needed different proof."

Why these 3: Someone who chose "Patient" is either practising patience with their partner right now, or craving it from them. These questions surface whether they hold space quietly or actively — and whether they expect the same from their partner.
Axis A: Inward patience = Expressive · Outward checking = Active Axis C: Same-pick prediction = they see their partner as a mirror → Protecting
🌿 Present
Intention: Present
They chose Present because something — work, phone, mental noise — is pulling them away from the relationship today. Questions live in the territory of attention, distraction, and what being truly there looks like for each person.
"You chose presence today. These 3 rounds are about what 'being there' actually means to you — and whether your partner would say the same thing."
Round 1
"On a weeknight at home together, phones should be…"
📵
In another room — this time belongs to us
Out of sight, out of reach, actually gone
VS
📲
Around, but I'm with you — I can do both
Trust, not rules
Axis A

📵 No phone = Expressive (deep, protective of space) · 📲 Trust = Active (relaxed, fluid)

Couple Card

"You need the phone gone. They just need you looking at them. You want the same thing — you just set different rules for it."

Round 2
"The kind of time together that makes me feel genuinely close…"
🛋️
Doing nothing together — same room, no agenda, just us
Parallel presence. No plan needed.
VS
🚶
Doing something together — a walk, a meal, shared focus
I feel closest when we're moving in the same direction
Axis A

🛋️ Still together = Expressive · 🚶 Moving together = Active

Couple Card

"You feel close by being still together. They feel close by doing something together. Both of you are saying — I just want us."

Round 3
"When I'm physically with my partner but mentally somewhere else…"
🙏
Don't call it out — let me find my way back
I know. I'm trying. I just need a minute.
VS
👋
Gently bring me back — "hey, you're somewhere else"
I'd rather be caught than drift further away
Axis A

🙏 Self-return = Expressive · 👋 Called back = Active (wants engagement)

Couple Card

"You want space to come back on your own. They want a gentle pull. Either way — you both want to be together."

Why these 3: "Present" is one of the most common intention words chosen — usually on days when screens, work, or life noise is winning. These questions surface what presence actually means to each person, which is rarely the same thing.
Axis A: Still/inward presence = Expressive · Active/doing presence = Active High mismatch expected on Round 3 — productive reveal

Highest emotional depth of all 8 sets · Round 3 frequently opens conversations that weren't happening
🧡 Warm
Intention: Warm
They chose Warm because they want to give or receive more tenderness today. Questions live in the territory of daily affection — how it's given, how it's received, and what it means to each person when it's absent.
"You chose warmth today. These 3 rounds are about how love shows up in the small moments — and whether your partner would recognise themselves in your answers."
Round 1
"The way I naturally show warmth to my partner on a regular day…"
🤏
Small touches — a hand on the back, a forehead kiss, without saying anything
My hands say it before my mouth does
VS
🗣️
Words — "I'm proud of you," "you looked really good today" — out loud
If I feel it, I want them to hear it
Axis A

🤏 Touch = Active (physical expression) · 🗣️ Words = Expressive (verbal depth)

Couple Card

"You reach. They say. Both of you are giving love — the other person just has to notice which language it comes in."

Round 2
"When my partner is having a hard day, the warmth they need from me is probably…"
☕
Just be near — make tea, sit with them, no fixing, no talking needed
Presence is the warmth
VS
🛠️
Help them do the thing they're dreading — take something off the list
Love is showing up and actually doing something
Axis A

☕ Sit near = Expressive (still warmth) · 🛠️ Do something = Active

Couple Card

"You offer presence. They offer action. What you both want is to feel less alone — and you give that to each other in completely different ways."

Round 3
"I feel most loved when my partner…"
🧠
Remembers something small I said weeks ago — and brings it up
"You mentioned this once…" That. That's it for me.
VS
🤗
Reaches for my hand in public — not when it's romantic, just automatically
The "you're mine and I'm not hiding it" kind
Axis A

🧠 Memory = Expressive (values emotional attentiveness) · 🤗 Touch = Active

Couple Card

"You want to be remembered. They want to be held. You've been giving each other exactly what you need — even if you didn't know the other one needed it too."

Why these 3: Warmth is about love languages without naming them. When the couple card reveals here, even a mismatch reads beautifully — "you love differently, not less."
Highest match-rate set after Set 1 · Strong Protecting signal when predictions align
